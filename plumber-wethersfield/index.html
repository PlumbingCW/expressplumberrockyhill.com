Do this before the plumber arrives

  1. Close the main shutoff valve, usually where the water line enters the basement near the meter.
  2. Turn off power to any room with standing water if you can reach the panel without stepping in the water. Otherwise stay out and call 911 if there is any risk of shock.
  3. Move rugs, boxes and electronics off the floor and open a drain or run a mop to keep water from spreading.
  4. If you smell gas, leave the building and call 911 and your gas utility's emergency number before you call us.

MDC water and sewer, and what we handle

Wethersfield is an MDC member town, so the Metropolitan District Commission supplies water and sewer service. The line from the street to your meter and the public main belong to the utility's side of the system. Everything inside your walls and on your property is yours to maintain.

If water floods the street or the whole block loses pressure, call MDC at (860) 278-7850. If the problem is inside your house or on your own service pipe, call us. Not sure which? Describe what you see and we will tell you honestly whether it sounds like a utility issue.

Before we arrive in Wethersfield

Four steps that limit damage while a plumber is on the way.

1

Find the main valve

In most older Wethersfield houses it sits on the basement wall by the meter. Turn it clockwise until it stops.

2

Clear a path

Move boxes, laundry and furniture away from the leak and the basement stairs so the plumber can reach the pipe.

3

Photograph the damage

Take a few pictures before you start cleaning. Many standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental damage but not gradual leaks. Policies vary.

4

Have the details ready

Know your address, the room involved and whether water is still flowing. It speeds up the call.
