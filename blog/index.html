When to stop reading and call

Guides cannot see your pipes. If you smell gas, see water near outlets, or have sewage on the floor, leave the area and get help first. For a burst pipe, close the main shutoff and call.

For anything else, the guides help you decide whether it is a quick fix or a job for a licensed plumber. You can read about our drain cleaning and water heater repair work on the service pages.
