What to know in each town

The pipes behind a call depend on the house and the utility, so these notes cover what usually matters.

Rocky Hill 06067

Most homes date from the 1960s to the 1980s, so older shutoff valves and water heaters are common causes of calls. MDC supplies water and sewer, so ask MDC about anything on its side of the meter.

Wethersfield 06109

About 68% of homes were built before 1970, so galvanized or old copper lines and original drains turn up. MDC serves water and sewer here too, which keeps the utility handoff the same as Rocky Hill.

Newington 06111

About 53% of homes were built before 1970, and most are detached single-family houses. MDC covers water and sewer. A basement floor drain that bubbles is a common reason to call.

Cromwell 06416

Cromwell is served by the Cromwell Fire District Water Department, a different system from MDC. Ask the district for its latest water quality report and test your tap before sizing a filter or softener.

How coverage is confirmed by address

ZIP codes alone do not decide coverage. A ZIP can span several areas, and our schedule changes through the day. So the dispatcher confirms by the actual street address when you call.

Give the address, the town and a one-line description of the problem. If a plumber is available, we book it and give you the price before work starts. If not, we say so at once and suggest what to do in the meantime.

Emergencies are handled ahead of routine jobs, and the line is open 24/7. Same-day appointments are offered when available. We do not promise an arrival time.
