Water you cannot see is still water you pay for. Our 24/7 line sends a licensed plumber to find where it is escaping, before it reaches your ceiling, floor or foundation.

If a ceiling is dripping, a floor is warm or soaked, or the meter keeps spinning with every tap closed, treat it as an emergency leak detection call. Our line is open 24/7, and a licensed plumber can often come the same day when schedules allow. Shut off the main valve, then call us while the house dries out.

Most leaks never announce themselves. They show up as a bill that doubled, a hiss in a quiet room or a patch of paint that will not stay on the wall. Finding the exact spot matters, because opening the wrong section of wall or slab costs far more than the repair itself.

When a hidden leak is an emergency right now

A slow drip behind a vanity can wait for a daytime visit. Water coming through a ceiling, pooling under a floor or running through a wall cannot. It soaks insulation and drywall, feeds mold and can reach wiring within hours.

Call right away if the meter spins with everything off, if a floor feels hot or damp over a concrete slab, or if you hear running water that no fixture explains. Each of those means water is escaping under pressure and will keep going until the line is shut.

While you wait for the plumber

  1. Close the main shutoff valve, usually where the water line enters the basement or utility room, and turn it clockwise until it stops.
  2. Open one faucet on the lowest floor to drain the pressure from the pipes.
  3. If water is near outlets, light fixtures or the electrical panel, leave the area and do not touch anything electrical while wet. Call 911 if you see sparks or smell burning.
  4. Move rugs, boxes and furniture out of the wet area and place a bucket under any dripping ceiling.
  5. Take photos of the damage and note the meter reading, then tell us when you call.

High water bills and the running meter

Your bill is the earliest warning most homeowners get. A toilet flapper that sticks open can waste more water in a month than a family uses in showers. Start there, because it is the cheapest cause to fix.

If the toilets are fine, do a meter test. Turn off every fixture, appliance and ice maker, then read the meter and read it again after an hour. Any movement means a leak somewhere. If it moves and the main valve inside the house is closed, the leak is between the meter and your valve, and our water line repair page covers that part of the system.

Slab leaks and wet ceilings

A slab leak is a pressurized supply line that has split under or inside a concrete floor. The signs are a warm floor, a hiss at night, a wet carpet edge or a damp baseboard on the ground level. Slab leaks do not fix themselves, and water can undermine soil beneath the floor over time.

Ceiling stains are tricky because water travels. It runs along a joist before it drops, so the stain can sit several feet from the pipe. We trace it back by testing each fixture above and checking the lines in the wall or floor cavity. If a pipe has already split, burst pipe repair is the follow-up visit.

How a plumber finds a leak without tearing the house apart

Pressure testing

We isolate sections of the supply system and watch the gauge. A drop that matches a section narrows the search to that run of pipe.

Listening equipment

Ground and wall microphones pick up the sound of water escaping a pipe, which helps pin down a spot under a floor or behind plaster.

Thermal imaging

A heat camera shows a hot water line leaking into a slab or a cold patch behind a wall. It reads surfaces and does not see through concrete.

Camera on drain lines

When the water is escaping from a drain rather than a supply pipe, a camera inspection shows cracks and failed joints from the inside.

Questions homeowners ask

Do you handle leak detection emergencies at night or on weekends in Rocky Hill?

Yes. The dispatch line answers 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Tell us where the water is showing and we book the first plumber who can reach your address. After-hours visits are priced differently from weekday ones, and we give you the price before work starts.

How do I know if my water meter is running because of a leak?

Turn off all water use, then read the meter and read it again after an hour. If the numbers change, water is escaping. Close your main valve and check again. If the meter stops, the leak is inside the house. If it keeps moving, it sits between the meter and the valve.

Can a leak under a concrete slab be fixed without breaking the floor?

Sometimes. Detection narrows the spot so any opening is small. Repair may mean a short cut in the slab, or rerouting the line through walls or the attic. The plumber explains the options and prices each one before work starts.

Why is my ceiling stained when the pipes above look dry?

Water travels along framing before it drops, so the stain is often away from the source. It can also be a roof leak or condensation on a duct. We test each fixture above the stain and check the roof side before suggesting a repair.

Will low water pressure always mean a leak?

No. A partly closed valve, a clogged aerator or a failing pressure regulator can cause it as well. A leak is likely if pressure drops across the whole house and the meter moves with everything off. Call and describe which taps are affected.
