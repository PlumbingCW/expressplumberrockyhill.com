Emergency Burst Pipe Repair in Rocky Hill, CT

A split pipe can put hundreds of gallons into a house in an hour. Stop the water first, then call our 24/7 line and a licensed plumber will take it from there.

If a pipe has burst, find the main shutoff and close it now, then call us. Our line is open 24/7, and a licensed plumber can often come the same day when schedules allow. Every minute the water runs adds to the damage, so the shutoff comes before the phone call, the photos and the cleanup.

Pipes burst for a few reasons. Water freezes and expands inside the line, old metal pipe corrodes through, or a fitting or valve gives out under pressure. Each one needs a different repair, so tell us what you saw and heard when you call.

What to do right now

01

Shut off the main water valve

It is usually on the wall where the water line enters the basement or a utility room. Turn the handle clockwise. A lever valve closes with a quarter turn across the pipe.

02

Open the lowest tap

Run a basement or ground floor faucet to drain the pipes. Flush upstairs toilets once to empty the tanks. Less water in the system means less leaking out of the break.

03

Cut power to wet areas only if it is safe

If water reaches outlets, appliances or the panel, do not wade in or touch anything electrical. Leave the house and call 911 if you see sparks or smell burning.

04

Turn off the water heater

An electric heater should be switched off at its breaker if you can reach it dry. For gas units, set the control to pilot or off. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 or the gas utility's emergency number.

05

Call us and describe the break

Tell us where the water is coming from, what the pipe is made of if you know, and whether the main valve closed. Then start protecting belongings.

Frozen pipes and thawing safely

If water has stopped coming from a tap in cold weather, a pipe may be frozen but not yet split. Leave the tap open and keep the heat on. Warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer on low, working from the faucet end toward the frozen spot. Water can then escape as it thaws.

Never use a torch, a propane heater or any open flame on a pipe. It can ignite framing or insulation and can crack a pipe that would otherwise survive. If you cannot find the frozen spot, or you see a bulge or a split, close the main valve and call. A pipe often shows the break only once ice melts.

Cleaning up after the water stops

Once the main is closed and the plumber is on the way, these first steps limit the damage.

Photograph everything

Take photos and video of the break, the wet areas and damaged items before moving anything. You may need them later.

Remove standing water

Use towels, a mop or a wet vacuum on small amounts. For a lot of water, a restoration company has pumps and dryers. Stay out of water near live electrical.

Lift what you can

Move rugs, boxes and furniture off wet floors. Pull wet carpet back from the wall so air gets underneath.

Run air, not guesses

Open windows if the weather allows and use fans. Drywall and insulation that stay wet for days can grow mold, so ask the restoration company what must come out.

What burst pipe repair costs

We give a clear price before work starts. These are the factors that shape it.

What moves the price

  • Where the break is: an exposed basement pipe is quicker than one behind a finished wall or ceiling.
  • The pipe material and size, and whether matching fittings are on hand.
  • How much pipe has to be replaced, not just the visible split.
  • Whether drywall or flooring must be opened and who restores it afterward.
  • Time of the call: nights, weekends and holidays are usually priced differently from a weekday visit.
Insurance note: many standard homeowners policies cover sudden and accidental water damage but not the worn part itself or gradual leaks. Policies vary, so read yours or call your agent soon after the pipe bursts.

Questions homeowners ask

Do you handle burst pipe emergencies at night or on weekends in Rocky Hill?

Yes. The dispatch line answers 24/7, including nights, weekends and holidays. Tell us where the water is coming from and we book the first plumber who can reach your address. After-hours visits are priced differently from weekday ones, and we give you the price before work starts.

Where is my main water shutoff?

It is usually on the wall where the water line enters the house, often in a basement, utility room or crawl space. It may have a round wheel handle or a lever. If you cannot find or close it, call us and we will talk you through the next step.

Is a frozen pipe always going to burst?

No. Many freeze and thaw without splitting. Risk goes up when ice sits for hours or when the pipe thaws and refreezes. Once it thaws, check the area for drips or stains, and call if you see water or hear it where no tap is running.

Should I call a plumber or a water damage company first?

Call the plumber first, because the water must stop before cleanup can work. A restoration company then removes water and dries the area. Your insurance agent can tell you what the policy expects and in what order.

Can a burst pipe be repaired without replacing the whole line?

Often yes, if the rest of the pipe is sound. A short section is cut out and replaced. If the pipe is badly corroded or has failed more than once, the plumber may suggest replacing a longer run. You decide after hearing the price.
