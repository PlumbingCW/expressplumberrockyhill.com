Cromwell's water comes from a different source

Rocky Hill is served by MDC reservoir water. Cromwell is not. Cromwell's supply is groundwater from the Gardiner wellfield, which is a different source from the MDC reservoirs that serve Rocky Hill. The Cromwell Fire District Water Department runs it.

For you, that matters if you are thinking about a softener or filter. Ask the Cromwell Fire District Water Department for its latest water quality report and test your tap before sizing equipment. Do not assume a neighbor in Rocky Hill has the same water.
